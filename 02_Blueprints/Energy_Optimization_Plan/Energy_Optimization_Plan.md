# Energy Optimization Plan

## 🏗️ Architecture / Workflow
A systematic approach to reducing monthly utility bills by targeting high-consumption devices and optimizing usage patterns.

## 📋 Checklist & Steps
1. **Appliance Audit**:
    - [ ] Check water heater temperature (Target: 120°F).
    - [ ] Monitor high-wattage appliances.
2. **Behavioral Optimization**:
    - [ ] Set winter thermostat to 68°F (Day) / 62°F (Night).
    - [ ] Schedule high-demand tasks for off-peak hours.
3. **Structural Maintenance**:
    - [ ] Check window and door seals for leaks.
    - [ ] Insulate exposed hot water pipes.
