# Home Energy Efficiency Principles

## 📖 Definition / Principle
The fundamental logic of how energy is consumed and saved in a residential environment, focusing on thermodynamics and appliance efficiency.

## 🔍 Core Details
- **Thermostat Delta**: Every 1°F reduction in heating temperature (in winter) correlates to approximately 3% savings in heating costs.
- **Water Heater Baseline**: Setting a water heater to 120°F (49°C) is the optimal balance between safety and energy efficiency.
- **Standby Power**: High-wattage appliances (Dehumidifiers, Space Heaters) can consume significant power and should be monitored for their duty cycle.
