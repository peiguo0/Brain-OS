# Utility Tiered Pricing Logic

## 📖 Definition / Principle
Many utility providers (e.g., electricity, water) use a tiered pricing structure where the cost per unit increases once a certain threshold is passed. This is designed to encourage conservation.

## 🔍 Core Details
- **Tier 1**: Baseline usage at a lower rate.
- **Tier 2**: Usage exceeding the baseline threshold, charged at a higher rate.
- **Additional Costs**:
    - **Surcharges**: Variable adjustments based on power costs.
    - **Taxes**: Municipal or state taxes calculated as a percentage of the total bill.
